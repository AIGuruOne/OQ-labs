# Peer scoring sheet (Day 5, S30 showcase, 65 minutes)

**Who:** every person scores every group except their own, alone, in
silence, in 90 seconds, straight after that group's demo. **Scores are
not discussed in the room.** Questions are about the build, never about
a score. The facilitator collects the sheets and reads out the medians
at the end.

## Clock

| Groups | Per group | Then |
|---|---|---|
| 4 or 5 | 11 min: demo 7, questions 2, silent scoring 2 | the rest of the 65: medians read out, one "steal this" per group |
| 6 | 9 min: demo 6, questions 1.5, silent scoring 1.5 | 11 min: medians, one "steal this" per group |

A demo that overruns stops at the bell; the group scores what was shown.
Each demo opens with the group's deployment-checklist verdict (one sentence).

## The six criteria (0 / 1 / 2 each, 12 in all)

Score what was **shown**, not what was said. When unsure between two
scores, take the lower one: the anchors are written so that a 2 is
visible from the audience.

| # | Criterion (the session it comes from) | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **It ran live** (S27-S28) | nothing ran, or only slides / screenshots | ran, but a FALLBACK or failure went unmentioned | ran live, and they said which pieces were theirs and which were pre-baked |
| 2 | **A number on unseen data** (S12, S19) | no number, or measured on training data | a number, but no comparison or no n | a number on held-out data, compared with a baseline, with its n and what it does not show |
| 3 | **A wrong answer has a path** (S24, S25) | the wrong case was not shown | described, not demonstrated | a wrong or hostile input shown being caught, refused or sent to a person |
| 4 | **Nothing writes without a person** (S26, S29) | a write happens with no approval, or nobody knows | writes are gated, but the approver or the log line was not shown | "no writes", or an approval shown with the named approver and its audit line |
| 5 | **Owned after Thursday** (S3-S5, S29) | no owner, no cost | owner or cost named, not both | a named owner, a cost per month with its source, and the model switch shown or explained |
| 6 | **Honest about the gaps** (all week) | claims more than was shown | gaps mentioned when asked | gaps named unprompted, each with who would close it |

## For the facilitator

- Read out the **median** per criterion per group, not the total and not
  a ranking. A column of 0s on criterion 3 across the room is the
  finding to close the week on, not a group's failure.
- Keep the "steal" column: it is the list OQ takes home.
- If two scorers differ by 2 on one criterion, the anchor was unclear
  for that demo. Note it for the next run; do not re-score.
