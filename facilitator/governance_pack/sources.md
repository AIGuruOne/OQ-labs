# Sources cited by the governance pack

Every framework, regulation and vendor document the eight templates
cite, with the link, what the pack takes from it, and how it was
verified. Checked 2026-09-22 by the build team. Nothing here is cited
from memory: "primary, opened" means the page or file itself was
fetched and read that day; "secondary" means a reputable summary was
read because the primary was paywalled or a PDF the tooling could not
parse. Re-check the dates the week before the room; the EU and MCP
entries move fastest.

Legal entries are pointers, not advice. Anything under [S5] goes to
OQ's data protection officer before it is relied on.

## What was deliberately not cited

- ISO/IEC 23894:2023 (AI risk management guidance) and NIST AI 100-2
  (adversarial machine learning taxonomy): relevant, but nothing in
  the templates depends on them and neither was opened.
- MITRE ATLAS: a threat catalogue, useful for a red-team exercise the
  week does not run.
- Any Oman sector-specific (energy, OT) regulation: none was verified,
  so none is named. Template 1's tier T3 sends anything touching plant
  operation to HSE and the OT owner instead.
- The EU AI Act's serious-incident reporting (Art. 73) and post-market
  monitoring (Art. 72): the templates borrow the *shape* of Art. 12
  and 14 only, because those two are self-contained checklists; the
  reporting articles presuppose an EU provider.
