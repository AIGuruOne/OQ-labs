# Requesting access to Tavrona ERP

## What this covers
Getting a **Tavrona ERP** account, or getting a role added to an account you already have.

## Access is by role, not by person
You do not ask for "access to Tavrona". You ask for a named role - for example *Requisitioner*,
*Goods Receipt* or *Maintenance Planner*. If you do not know which role you need, ask the person
whose work you are taking over and use theirs.

## Steps
1. Open **StaffGate** and choose *Request system access*.
2. Pick Tavrona ERP and the role. StaffGate shows who approves that role.
3. Your line manager approves first, then the role owner. Both approvals are required and neither
   can be skipped.
4. Provisioning runs overnight. You can sign in the next working morning.

## Timing
Allow two working days. A request raised on Thursday afternoon is usually usable on Sunday. There
is no same-day path, including for month-end - raise it before you need it.

## If sign-in fails after provisioning
Sign out of everything and back in once: Tavrona reads your roles at sign-in. If it still fails,
raise a ticket to `erp_support` with the role name from your StaffGate request.
