# AssetHive shows the wrong laptop against your name

## Symptom
**AssetHive** lists an asset tag against your name that is not the laptop you have, or lists none
at all.

## Why it matters
AssetHive decides who gets a replacement, who gets software licences, and what is written off. A
wrong record usually surfaces at the worst moment - when you need a replacement urgently.

## Steps
1. Read the tag off the underside of the laptop you actually have: `LAP-04412` form.
2. Raise a ticket to `end_user_computing` with both tags - the one you have and the one AssetHive
   shows.
3. The desk corrects the record. You cannot edit AssetHive yourself, and neither can your manager.

## Do not
Do not swap laptops with a colleague informally. It makes two records wrong instead of one, and
the second is found months later by someone who cannot ask you about it.
