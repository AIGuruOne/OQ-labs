# Getting a ticket treated as urgent at month end

## The honest answer
"It is month end" does not raise a ticket's priority on its own. Priority follows the stated
business effect: who is blocked, from doing what, and by when. Month end matters because it
creates that effect - so describe the effect, not the calendar.

## What actually raises priority
- A named deadline with a date and time.
- The number of people who cannot work, and which site or team.
- Whether a workaround exists, and what it costs.

## What does not
Capitals, exclamation marks, and the word URGENT in the subject. The desk reads the body.

## An example that works
*"Goods receipt in Tavrona ERP is refused for all four users in the MRB warehouse team. Month-end
close is Thursday 14:00. No workaround - postings cannot be entered by hand."*

That is a `high` ticket on its own facts. A ticket saying "URGENT!!! month end!!!" with no detail
is triaged as `medium` and asked for the detail, which costs a day.
