# Expired password on MyPortal or Windows

## Symptom
Sign-in is refused and the message mentions an expired or out-of-date password. This is the
commonest single call the desk takes.

## Who can fix it
You can, without raising a ticket, as long as your account is not locked.

## Steps
1. Go to **MyPortal** and choose *Change my password* on the sign-in screen.
2. Enter the old password once and the new one twice. The new password must be at least 14
   characters and cannot repeat any of your last five.
3. Lock and unlock your laptop once so **Windows** picks up the new password.
4. If you use a desk phone, sign in again on **VoxLine** as well.

## If it does not work
Three wrong attempts locks the account for 30 minutes. Wait it out rather than retrying: each
retry restarts the 30 minutes. If it is still refused after the lock clears, raise a ticket to
`identity_access` and say which of the four steps failed.

## Passwords expire every 90 days
MyPortal warns you on sign-in for the last 14 days. Change it when first warned - not on the
morning of a deadline.
