# Reporting a suspicious email

Also covers: phishing, spoofed senders, and messages pretending to be from the IT desk.

## What to do
Use the **Report suspicious** button in **Outlook**. It is on the message toolbar. That single
click sends the message with its full headers to `security_ops` and removes it from your inbox.

## Do not
- Do not forward a suspicious message to anyone, including the desk. Forwarding strips the headers
  that identify the sender, which is the part `security_ops` needs.
- Do not reply, and do not click a link "to see where it goes".
- Do not warn colleagues by forwarding it. Tell `security_ops`, who will warn everyone properly.

## If you already clicked
Report it anyway, immediately, and say that you clicked. Nobody is in trouble for clicking; people
are in trouble for not saying so. Speed decides how much work the response is.

## If you entered your password
Change it at once on **MyPortal**, then report it. In that order.
