# Access after a transfer or role change

## Symptom
You have transferred to a new team and either cannot reach what you now need, or can still reach
what you no longer should.

## Transfers do not move your access
**StaffGate** updates your reporting line automatically. It does not add or remove system roles.
Your old access stays until someone removes it, and your new access does not exist until someone
requests it. Both halves are somebody's job, and that somebody is usually you.

## What to do on day one
1. List the systems your new role needs. Your new manager or the person you replaced knows them.
2. Raise one StaffGate request per system, naming the role.
3. In the same request, list the old roles you should lose. This is the half everybody forgets,
   and it is the half the auditors look at.

## Why it matters
Access that outlives a transfer is the single commonest finding in an access review. Removing it
is not a formality.
