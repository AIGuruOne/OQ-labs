# CORP-WIFI keeps dropping on site

## Symptom
**CORP-WIFI** disconnects repeatedly, or shows connected with no traffic, in one part of a site.

## First, establish whether it is you or the area
Ask one person sitting near you whether they see it too. The answer changes who fixes it and how
fast. A single laptop is `end_user_computing`; an area is `network_ops`.

## What to include in the ticket
Coverage problems are fixed from patterns, so vague reports go nowhere. Give:
- the site code and the room or area,
- the times it dropped, to the nearest ten minutes,
- how many people are affected,
- whether a cabled connection in the same room is stable.

## What will not help
Forgetting and re-joining the network repeatedly. It clears a stale credential once, and after
that it only hides the pattern the desk needs to see.

## Guest wi-fi is not a workaround
Guest wi-fi cannot reach internal systems, by design, and moving to it while a fault is open
usually stops the desk being able to reproduce the problem.
