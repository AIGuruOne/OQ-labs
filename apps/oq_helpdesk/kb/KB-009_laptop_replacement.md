# Replacing a laptop, and what happens to your data

## When you can get a replacement
Laptops are replaced on a four-year cycle, or sooner if a fault cannot be repaired. Being slow is
not itself a fault - raise a ticket and let the desk measure it.

## Steps
1. Find your asset tag. It is on the underside of the laptop in the form `LAP-04412`.
2. Raise a ticket to `end_user_computing` with the tag and the fault.
3. The desk checks the record in **AssetHive** and either books a repair or raises a replacement.
4. Replacements are handed over in person at your site. They are not posted between sites.

## Your data is your responsibility until handover
Anything saved only on the laptop is lost when it is wiped, and it is wiped the same day it is
returned. Move your files to **DocHarbor** or the `S: drive` first. The desk does not image the
old disk and cannot recover it afterwards.

## Loaner laptops
A loaner is available for up to five working days when a repair will take longer. Ask for one in
the same ticket - the desk does not offer it unprompted.
